# iOS attachment entry patterns in open-source apps

Research date: 2026-09-27. These are source-code observations from four current development revisions, not a survey of shipped App Store builds. Code establishes controls and routes; descriptions of their exact on-device appearance are inferences unless a first-party screenshot is cited.

## Recommendation and implementation

Keep separate Photos, Files, and Camera destinations. The sampled apps do too. Snip Snap has since adopted a `Menu` attached to the Add button, with icon-labeled actions that open its existing Photos and Files pickers or Camera when available. This is a design choice, not a platform requirement. Before the change, Snip Snap used a SwiftUI `confirmationDialog`, also a system control; Joplin uses a similar two-step source choice. ([previous Snip Snap source menu](https://github.com/sreejithraman/snip-snap/blob/e0aecd903425f754194bed554856233c9f810ce8/SnipSnapiOS/AttachmentMediaImport.swift), [current Snip Snap source menu](../../SnipSnapiOS/AttachmentMediaImport.swift), [Snip Snap picker routing](../../SnipSnapiOS/EditorViews.swift), [Element X menu](https://github.com/element-hq/element-x-ios/blob/9205e11193ec58aea763434dfd2610e898fda1a3/ElementX/Sources/Screens/RoomScreen/ComposerToolbar/View/RoomAttachmentPicker.swift), [Nextcloud plus menu](https://github.com/nextcloud/ios/blob/c7659ccf417208bf43b33bde29b2792aea362579/iOSClient/Menu/NCContextMenuPlus.swift), [Joplin command](https://github.com/laurent22/joplin/blob/4c0e2bc0e221e934023b20f462f035aa3321f4b5/packages/app-mobile/components/screens/Note/commands/attachFile.ts))

## Synthesis and limits

Across these four implementations, the shared pattern is an app-owned **source choice** followed by a source-specific picker or camera flow. There is no common first-screen layout: two use compact platform menus, one shows a dialog, and one has a custom attachment keyboard. This small, purposeful sample does not establish what most iOS apps use, and it does not prove the exact look of any shipped version. It establishes that Snip Snap's extra source-choice step was ordinary; the original dialog's presentation was the likely visual difference.
